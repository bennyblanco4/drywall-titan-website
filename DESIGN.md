## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-trust, premium contracting presence for both large-scale commercial developments and high-end residential interiors. Moving decisively away from casual, unvetted contractor stereotypes, the interface projects industrial precision, licensed professionalism, structural integrity, and elite surface craftsmanship.

The visual style blends **Corporate Modern** with **Architectural Minimalism**:
- **Monolithic Authority:** Grounded deep blues and charcoal steel surfaces mirror heavy-duty framing, troweled finishes, and structural permanence.
- **Precision Detailing:** Laser-calibrated spacing, sharp structural dividers, and tight data displays echo Level 5 drywall finishing—smooth, spotless, and dustless.
- **Confidence & Validation:** Trust seals, licensing verifications, guarantee indicators, and verified metrics are treated as primary visual anchors rather than afterthoughts.

## Colors

The palette is rooted directly in the structural materials of tradecraft: galvanized steel, deep titan navy, and refined architectural neutrals.

- **Primary (`#0A2540` - Deep Titan Navy):** Anchors main headers, primary calls to action, hero background blocks, and authoritative navigation bars. Emphasizes stability, commercial capacity, and unwavering reliability.
- **Secondary (`#2C3437` - Steel Armor Charcoal):** Derived from steel framing and trowel blades. Used for body text, structural card headers, sub-navigation, and secondary button borders.
- **Tertiary (`#1D70B8` - Electric Precision Blue):** High-contrast actionable hue used for focused states, interactive buttons, dynamic quotes, and key metric callouts.
- **Accent Trust Gold (`#D97706`):** Reserved strictly for guarantee shields, 5-star ratings, warranty verification badges, and accreditation seals.
- **Neutral Palette:**
  - `Surface Base`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `Surface Subdued`: `#F8FAFC` (Architectural Gypsum White)
  - `Surface Contrast`: `#F1F5F9` (Trowel Slate Light)
  - `Border Subtle`: `#E2E8F0`
  - `Border Strong`: `#CBD5E1`
  - `Text Muted`: `#64748B`

## Typography

The type scale combines **Montserrat** for display and section headers with **Inter** for transactional workflows, structural forms, and body clarity.

- **Headlines (Montserrat):** Set in strong, geometric, architectural uppercase or title case. The heavy weights (700/800) echo the solid block type from the company wordmark, conveying industrial power and certainty.
- **Body & Data (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and structurally balanced. Ensures complex bids, scope checklists, project timelines, and warranty terms can be reviewed effortlessly across all device sizes.
- **Badges & Trust Tags:** Rendered in uppercase bold Montserrat with wide letter spacing (`letterSpacing: 0.08em`) to mimic stamped structural certification plates and official seal typography.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a rigid 12-column desktop grid with a maximum content boundary of `1280px`, scaling down to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile.

- **Layout Grid Rules:**
  - Content containers conform to a crisp, centered frame with substantial outer safe margins (`margin: 3rem` on desktop, reducing to `1.25rem` on mobile screens).
  - Columns utilize explicit, sharp gutters (`1.5rem` desktop / `1rem` mobile) ensuring cards and specification tables retain defined separation.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:**
  - Structural spacing follows an exact base-8 modular rhythm (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `40px`, `64px`).
  - Section blocks utilize `space-2xl` (`4rem` to `6rem`) vertical padding to create breathable, premium white space reminiscent of luxury architectural catalogs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids excessive blur or floating neomorphism, preferring the crisp edges of planar construction surfaces and solid architectural materials.

- **Base Layer (Flat Slate):** Cards and content modules rest flat against `#F8FAFC`, delimited by clean `1px solid #E2E8F0` borders.
- **Surface Elevation (Level 1):** Interactive cards, pricing modules, and calculators lift subtly using a sharp, low-spread ambient shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.06), 0 4px 12px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.04);`
- **Raised Elevation (Level 2):** Hovered project cards, modal overlays, and sticky instant-estimate drawers utilize a high-fidelity grounding shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.04);`
- **Solid Contrast Sections:** Dark-themed sections (Hero, Trust Statement, Master Warranty) utilize solid, zero-shadow `#0A2540` navy with sharp hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), projecting industrial fortitude.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft-Chiseled** shape language (`roundedness: 1`), keeping corner radii small and disciplined (`4px` to `8px`). 

- **Structural Components:** Inputs, buttons, and badges use `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radiuses, reflecting the machined angles of industrial drywall sheets, metal corner beads, and steel trowels.
- **Containers & Modals:** Large cards and before/after comparison frames use `8px` (`0.5rem`) radiuses maximum.
- **Strict Exclusions:** Circular pill shapes and bubbly curves are strictly avoided to ensure the aesthetic remains grounded, architectural, and commercial-grade.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action (Titan CTA):** Deep Navy (`#0A2540`) or Electric Blue (`#1D70B8`) background, white text, bold Montserrat typography, `4px` border radius, and explicit interior padding (`12px 24px`). Hover states darken background by 8% and lift by 1px with no outline softening.
- **Secondary / Technical:** Crisp white background with `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, Steel Armor Charcoal text (`#2C3437`), and subtle hover transition to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Estimate / Phone Direct:** Inverted high-contrast buttons featuring an inline phone icon and estimated time indicator.

### Trust Badges & Guarantees
- Compact pill-less rectangular tokens with `2px` left-accent borders (`#D97706` for Gold Guarantees; `#1D70B8` for Licensed & Insured verifications).
- Includes structured icons (Titan Spartan Crest, Shield, Verified Check, 5-Star Rating block).

### Input Fields & Instant Quote Calculators
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` encased in a crisp `1px solid #CBD5E1` border. 
- Focus State: Border color transforms instantly to `#1D70B8` with an offset `2px` ring of `rgba(29, 112, 184, 0.15)`.
- Helper text is pinned below in `caption` font weight, providing transparent explanations (e.g., "Level 4 vs Level 5 Drywall Finish requirements").

### Specification & Comparison Cards
- Clean slate containers (`#FFFFFF`) with `1px solid #E2E8F0` perimeter borders.
- Header bands are optionally clad in subtle `#F1F5F9` to segregate metadata (square footage, project type, commercial/residential, time to completion).
- Interactive before/after split sliders for dustless sanding demonstrations and Level 5 skim coating comparisons.

### Verified Testimonial Tiles
- Built with a rigid top border accent in Deep Navy, featuring verified client labels, project location, contract value scope, and precise quote excerpts.
