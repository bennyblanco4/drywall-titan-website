Frequently Asked Questions

Everything GTA homeowners need to know before initiating a restoration work order.

Do you both patch AND paint to match, or do I need a separate painter? expand_more
We handle both right in the same visit. That is what sets Drywall Titan apart from casual mudding contractors. We show up, install structural backing, compound, sand with HEPA vacuums, prime, and computer-match your paint using premium Sherwin-Williams coatings. One trade, one appointment, zero coordination headaches.
How do you prevent drywall dust from invading my home? expand_more
We implement medical-grade containment. Before a blade touches your drywall, we tape down heavy Ram Board along all walking pathways, seal off doorways with commercial magnetic zip-walls, and run commercial 99.97% HEPA air filtration equipment to create slight negative pressure, ensuring dust cannot migrate into your HVAC ducting.
Can you repair holes left behind by electricians and plumbers? expand_more
Yes, this accounts for over 40% of our daily calls across Toronto. Subtrades often cut wide access holes to run modern Romex wiring, heat pumps, or PEX piping. We install rigid timber backing studs, fasten new gypsum panels flush with your current wall plane, tape, mud, and feather the joints so the repair is completely invisible.
How much does drywall repair typically cost in the GTA? expand_more
Most single-day repairs range from $320 CAD for a simple doorknob impact patch up to $650 - $950 CAD for multi-hole electrical trenching and ceiling water remediation with color matching. Popcorn ceiling eradication is quoted on square footage with flat transparent tiering. Check our interactive calculator above for an immediate ballpark.
What is covered under the 1-Year Zero-Crack Warranty? expand_more
Every restoration performed by Drywall Titan is guaranteed for a full 12 months. If any thermal settlement crack reappears along our patched seam, or if joint tape bubbles or peels, we dispatch a technician within 48 hours to correct and repaint the area at zero expense to you.
