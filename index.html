Good to know

Questions people ask

A few things homeowners usually want to know first.

Do you patch and paint, or do I need a painter? expand_more
We do both in the same visit. We patch, sand, prime, and match your paint. You do not need to book a second person.
Will my house fill up with dust? expand_more
We cover the floors, seal the doorway, and run a filter so dust stays in the work area.
Can you fix holes from a plumber or electrician? expand_more
Yes. That is a big part of our work. We put backing behind the hole, patch it, and paint it so you cannot see it.
How much does this cost? expand_more
A small hole often starts around $320. Bigger jobs, like several holes or a ceiling leak with paint matching, are usually $650 to $950. Popcorn removal is priced by the size of the room. The calculator above gives you a rough number.
What does the 1-year warranty cover? expand_more
If our patch cracks, or the tape bubbles, within a year, we come back and fix it at no charge.
