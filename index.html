FAQ

Common questions

A few things homeowners usually ask before booking.

Do you handle painting, or will I need a painter? expand_more
Both are included. We patch, sand, prime, and colour-match your paint in the same visit, so there's no second trade to schedule.
How do you keep dust under control? expand_more
We cover the floors, seal off the doorway, and run air filtration so dust stays in the work area.
Can you repair holes left by plumbers or electricians? expand_more
Absolutely, it's one of our most common jobs. We install backing behind the opening, patch it, and paint it so it disappears.
How much does a repair cost? expand_more
Small hole repairs typically start around $320. Larger jobs, like multiple holes or a ceiling leak with paint matching, usually run $650 to $950. Popcorn removal is priced by room size. Use the estimator above for a quick number.
What does the 1-year warranty cover? expand_more
If our patch cracks or the tape lifts within a year, we'll return and repair it at no charge.
